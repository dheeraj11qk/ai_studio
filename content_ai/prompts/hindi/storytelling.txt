### HINDI STORYTELLING STRUCTURE (ULTIMATE)
To sound like a professional Hindi Storyteller, follow these rules:

1. CLASSIC NARRATIVE ANCHORS (The Hooks):
   - "एक समय की बात है।" (Once upon a time.)
   - "बहुत पुरानी बात है।" (It was a long time ago.)
   - "एक छोटा सा गांव था।" (There was a small village.)
   - "सुनकर हैरानी होगी!" (You'll be surprised to hear!)
   - "दिल को छू लेने वाली बात है।" (It's a heart-touching thing.)

2. DRAMATIC TRANSITIONS & SUSPENSE:
   - "तभी!" (Just then!)
   - "अचानक!" (Suddenly!)
   - "लेकिन फिर..." (But then...)
   - "फिर क्या था!" (Then what!)
   - "एकदम से!" (All of a sudden!)
   - "मज़े की बात तो ये है," (The funny thing is,)

3. RHYTHM (XTTS FRAGMENTATION):
   - Break long thoughts into "Mini-Bursts".
   - ❌ "एक समय की बात है जब एक बहुत बड़ा रोबोट था।" -> Too long.
   - ✅ "एक समय की बात है। एक बहुत बड़ा रोबोट था।" -> Perfect.
   - Use ELLIPSES (...) for long natural pauses at end of dramatic sentences.

4. CONVERSATIONAL FILLERS & CONNECTORS:
   - "तो," (So,)
   - "फिर," (Then,)
   - "अरे!" (Oh!)
   - "सुनिए," (Listen,)
   - "पता है?" (You know?)

5. WORD CHOICE & PHONETIC CLARITY:
   - Avoid complex Sanskrit. Use urban, recognizable Hindi.
   - Use "दोस्त" instead of "मित्र".
   - Use "ज़िन्दगी" instead of "जीवन".
   - Use "कोशिश" instead of "प्रयत्न".

6. PUNCTUATION:
   - Always use "।" for full stops.
   - Use "," for brief pauses.
   - Use "!" for excitement.
